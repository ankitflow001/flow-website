About Flowtonne

Data measured in tonnes, moving like a falcon

The name says it plainly: flow plus tonne. Data arrives in huge volumes and it has to keep moving. Our mark is the peregrine falcon, the fastest animal on Earth, because speed at scale is the whole point of the platform.

Flowtonne brings ingestion, stream processing, storage and query together. Pipelines that used to need five tools and a week of glue code become a few lines of configuration.

From kilobytes to quettabytes, the same platform grows with you.

How it works

From source to insight in four steps

Every pipeline in Flowtonne follows the same path, so new teams pick it up quickly.

  1. ConnectPoint Flowtonne at your sources: Kafka topics, Postgres tables, S3 buckets or REST endpoints.
  2. ShapeClean, join and aggregate with SQL or code. Changes deploy without stopping the stream.
  3. StoreResults land in tiered storage: hot for live queries, cold for cheap long-term history.
  4. ServeFeed dashboards, ML models and apps through SQL, APIs and live subscriptions.
